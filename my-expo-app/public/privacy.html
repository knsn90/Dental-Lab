2. Personal Data We Process

3. Purposes of Processing

  • Production, tracking and delivery of dental-lab work orders
  • Invoicing, collection and current-account management
  • Fulfilling legal obligations and maintaining security

4. Payment Security

Online payments are processed by the iyzico payment gateway over 256-bit SSL. We do not store your card details on Siman servers; they are processed by the PCI-DSS compliant payment institution.

5. Data Sharing / Transfer

Personal data may be shared with infrastructure providers (cloud hosting, payment processor, courier service) and legally authorized authorities only to the extent necessary to deliver the service and in accordance with the law.

6. Processing by Artificial Intelligence

Invoice / receipt and work-order text extraction (OCR) and the Simanty assistant use the third-party AI provider Anthropic (Claude) to analyze content you submit. Data sent to the provider: text on the document or image, patient name, date of birth and gender from the work order, order details and dentist info.

  • Data is transmitted encrypted and is not used by the provider for model training.
  • Transfer only occurs based on the explicit in-app consent you provide; the consent record is kept together with its version.
  • If you do not consent, AI features stay off and the rest of the app continues to work.
  • You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → Privacy.
  • Because the provider is located outside Türkiye, the transfer is based on your explicit consent.

7. 3D Face Scanning (TrueDepth / ARKit)

For smile design in the iOS app, face scanning uses the device's TrueDepth front camera and Apple ARKit to capture the patient's 3D face geometry and color face images. Scanning only runs when started by the user.

  • Face data is used solely for the prosthesis / smile design in the related work order. It is never used for authentication, face recognition, advertising, marketing, profiling or data mining.
  • The scan file is attached to the work order and only the lab and the clinic/dentist of that order can view it.
  • Face data is never sent to AI services or any other third party; it is only stored encrypted in our hosting infrastructure.
  • It is retained with the work-order record; you can request its deletion under section 9 (your rights).
  • The dentist / clinic performing the scan is responsible for obtaining the patient's explicit consent.

8. Location, Camera and Device Permissions (Android / iOS)

  • Location: For delivery tracking in the courier module (only courier role, only during work hours).
  • Camera: Work-order photos, QR scanning, and 3D face scanning (iOS).
  • Photos / Files: To attach documents to a work order.
  • Notifications: Order stage changes, delivery status, payment reminders.

Permissions can be revoked at any time from device settings. Denying a permission disables only the related feature; the rest of the app keeps working.

9. Retention and Security

Data is kept for the period required by the purpose of processing and the law. It is protected by technical and organizational measures including role-based access (row-level security), encryption, and audit logging.

10. Your Rights (KVKK art. 11 / GDPR art. 15–22)

You can exercise your rights — access, rectification, erasure, objection to processing and the other rights granted by law — by contacting nexadentlab@gmail.com. Data request and account deletion are also available in-app from your account.
